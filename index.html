Hero motion test

Two moments, five ways each.

The two from the last round: Speak Up, at scale, for the hero, and Line of Sight, for the second moment. Each one five ways, each a short story told by scroll and built on the classic principles of animation: a small move back before every big move, an overshoot and settle when things land, shapes that stretch with your speed and squash when they stop, paths that curve, and a follow-through when you stop scrolling. Line of Sight is now built from the line itself, written in Morse code on one grid: a dot is one unit, a dash three, and nothing floats. Signals key on, grow a unit at a time, or step along one line and lock. The lines along the way come from the brand book. The waveform studies from the last round are here too: in each, the headline's red full stop drops into the bar wall and changes it. Open an option and scroll slowly; the numbers at the bottom switch between them.
